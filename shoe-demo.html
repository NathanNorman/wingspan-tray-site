1. Upright, pinch from the top

Cards lean back 20°. The front wall is low, so you pinch the front card's top edge and lift it out. Lid over the back half only.

2. Upright, dealing-shoe throat

Like a casino shoe: push the front card down; its bottom edge goes out through the throat and it tips forward and slides out flat over the lip. Needs tuning prints.

3. Sideways, pinch from the top

Same as 1 with each card turned on its long edge: a lower, wider box.

4. Lying almost flat

Cards rest on a 15° ramp; slide the top card forward off the stack, over the low front wall.

Each box is cut in half to show inside. All hold 349 cards (half the collection). The orange card shows how one is drawn. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
