Play position: the sleeve covers one edge of every column, so the cubes stay in.

Packed away: the bearing ring sits in the storage base's pocket and the quarters rest on the base's walls; the birdfeeder lies on its side in its cradle; the dice sit beside it; the drum's goal tiles are lowered inside it. Four lids click onto the rims and the base, level across the top. The base holds the spare bird cards, Automa and swift-start cards, extra dice, eggs and action cubes.
