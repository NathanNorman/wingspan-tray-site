Option 1: flat platform (bridge)

The lift and drum print together. The platform's flat bottom is printed over a 0.3 mm air gap, so the printer has to span 27 mm across thin air. It can sag and stick to the floor; you'd push the tab down hard once to break it free.

Option 2: V-shaped bottom

Same lift, but its bottom is a 45° V sitting in a matching V in the pocket floor. Every surface prints at 45° or steeper, so nothing spans air and nothing should stick. The pocket goes about 14 mm deeper to fit the V.
